第 1 题 （考点：文化术语补全）
The Chinese concept of "seeking truth from ______" has been a guiding principle for both governance and academic research for decades.
A practice
B facts
C reality
D truth
解析："实事求是"的官方译法是 seeking truth from facts，这是固定文化术语。practice、reality、truth 都是字面直译的干扰项。
第 2 题 （考点：动词+介词搭配）
Traditional Chinese culture has much to offer ______ the building of a harmonious society and the promotion of global cultural exchange.
A for
B to
C in
D toward
解析：offer to 是固定搭配，表示"为……做出贡献、对……有用"。offer sth. to sth. 表示为某事提供某物。
第 3 题 （考点：形近词辨析）
The government has launched a series of projects to ______ and revitalize traditional crafts that are on the verge of extinction.
A preserve
B reserve
C deserve
D observe
解析：preserve 意为"保护、保存"，preserve traditional crafts 表示保护传统工艺，符合句意。reserve 是"预订；保留"，deserve 是"值得"，observe 是"观察；遵守"，均不符合。
第 4 题 （考点：词性/词形判断）
The ______ popularity of hanfu (traditional Han clothing) among young people reflects a growing sense of cultural confidence.
A increase
B increasing
C increased
D increasingly
解析：空格修饰名词 popularity，需要填入形容词。increasing（adj. 日益增长的）表示正在增长的过程，符合语境。increase 是动词/名词，increased 是"已增长的"，increasingly 是副词，均不符合语法。
第 5 题 （考点：文化术语补全）
The principle of "harmony but not ______" is a core concept in traditional Chinese thought that remains highly relevant today.
A sameness
B uniformity
C equality
D similarity
解析："和而不同"的标准译法是 harmony but not uniformity，uniformity 表示"一致性、统一性"。sameness 意为"相同"，equality 意为"平等"，similarity 意为"相似"，均不是标准译法。

Another important factor is public of the value of cultural soft power. For a long time, cultural development was often seen as secondary to economic growth. This perception is gradually changing, creating a more favorable environment which traditional culture can flourish in new forms.

Cultural confidence is not about closing the door to the outside world; it is about standing firmly on one's own cultural ground while engaging with others. Cultural confidence is not about looking back at the past with nostalgia, but about building a bridge between ancient wisdom and modern life. For a long time, traditional Chinese culture was often presented as something static and distant, something to be admired in museums but disconnected from daily reality. This narrow understanding is now being challenged by a new generation of creators who are finding fresh and creative ways to bring tradition into the present.

The revival of interest in traditional culture recognizes that the past is not a burden but a source of strength. It means reinterpreting classical philosophy for contemporary readers, integrating traditional aesthetics into modern design, and promoting cultural education in schools and communities so that young people can grow up with a deep appreciation for their heritage. At the same time, it emphasizes the importance of openness and exchange, ensuring that cultural confidence does not lead to isolation but to greater mutual understanding with the world.

Traditional Chinese thought has much to offer the global community. Concepts such as "harmony but not uniformity," "the unity of knowledge and action," and "all people under heaven are one family" provide valuable perspectives for addressing today's global challenges. When cultural traditions are kept alive through creative reinterpretation, they do not just connect people to their history — they offer shared wisdom for building a better future for all.

Cultural confidence is a more fundamental, deeper, and more enduring force in the development of a country and a nation. China's fine traditional culture is the spiritual lifeline of the Chinese nation, an important source for cultivating core socialist values, and a solid foundation on which we can stand firm amid the surging tides of world cultures.

In recent years, the rise of the "China chic" has become a remarkable cultural phenomenon. From hanfu (traditional Han clothing) and guofeng music to cultural and creative products, a growing number of young people have fallen in love with traditional culture, carrying it forward and innovating in their own ways. The Palace Museum's cultural merchandise, Henan TV's "Banquet in the Tang Palace," and live broadcasts of Sanxingdui archaeological excavations have repeatedly broken into the mainstream, making traditional culture more accessible and relatable to the public.

It is worth mentioning that the pace of culture going global has also been accelerating. Cultural activities such as Confucius Institutes, Chinese film festivals, and Spring Festival celebrations are widely popular around the world, showcasing the unique charm of Chinese culture to the global audience. It can be said that cultural confidence has not only enhanced national cohesion but also built a bridge for exchanges and mutual learning among different civilizations.

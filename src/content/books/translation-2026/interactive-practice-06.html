选词填空

第 1 题 （考点：动词+介词搭配）
Artificial intelligence is gradually penetrating ______ every aspect of people's lives, from smart home devices to personalized education.
A into
B through
C across
D over
解析：penetrate into 是固定搭配，表示"渗透进入"。penetrate through 是"穿透"，across 是"横跨"，over 是"越过"，均不符合。
第 2 题 （考点：形近词辨析）
The government has introduced new regulations to ensure that data is used responsibly and that people's privacy rights are properly ______.
A deserved
B reserved
C preserved
D observed
解析：preserve 意为"保护、维护"，preserve privacy rights 表示保护隐私权，符合句意。deserve 是"值得"，reserve 是"预订；保留"，observe 是"观察；遵守"，均不符合。
第 3 题 （考点：动词短语搭配）
China's digital economy has been growing rapidly, with e-commerce, mobile payments, and cloud computing all ______ significant progress in recent years.
A making
B doing
C taking
D having
解析：make progress 是固定搭配，表示"取得进步/进展"。这里是 with 复合结构，主动关系用现在分词 making。
第 4 题 （考点：词性/词形判断）
The ______ development of 5G technology has laid a solid foundation for the construction of smart cities and the industrial Internet.
A accelerate
B acceleration
C accelerated
D accelerating
解析：空格修饰名词 development，需要填入形容词。accelerating（adj. 加速发展中的）表示正在进行的动作，符合语境。accelerate 是动词，acceleration 是名词，accelerated 是"已加速的"，表示已完成状态。
第 5 题 （考点：科技术语补全）
The concept of "digital ______" refers to the gap between those who have access to information and communication technology and those who do not.
A division
B divide
C separation
D gap
解析："数字鸿沟"的标准译法是 digital divide，这是固定科技术语。digital gap 是字面翻译，不是标准表达。division 和 separation 都是干扰项。

spread v. show v. advance v. lack n. offer v. aware adj. in prep. but conj. promote v. impress v. significant adj. with prep. instead adv. that pron. rapid adj.

spreading
现在进行时：is spreading across the nation，is 后用现在分词构成进行时。

showing
现在分词作后置定语：data showing... 修饰 data，主动关系。

With
介词 with 短语表条件/伴随：with a strong foundation in...。注意首字母大写。

impressive
形容词：修饰 results，表示令人印象深刻的成果。impress 变形为形容词。
5
lack
名词：the lack of 表示"……的缺乏"。

offering
现在分词作伴随状语：government has launched programs, offering... 主动关系。

awareness
名词：public awareness 表示公众意识，aware 变形为名词 awareness。
8
in
介词：in which 引导定语从句，相当于 innovation can thrive in this environment。
9
but
连词：not just... but... 结构，表示"不仅仅是……而是……"。
10
promotes

The digital transformation of China is not about replacing human judgment with algorithms, but about using technology to augment human capabilities and create new opportunities for growth. For many years, digital innovation was often seen as a threat to traditional industries and employment, something that would automate jobs and leave people behind. This narrow view of technology is now being replaced by a more balanced understanding that recognizes both the challenges and the tremendous opportunities that digitalization brings.

The rise of the digital economy recognizes that technology is a tool that can be shaped by human values and social priorities. It means investing in digital infrastructure, promoting digital literacy, and creating regulatory frameworks that protect people's rights while encouraging innovation. At the same time, it emphasizes the importance of digital inclusion, ensuring that the benefits of the digital age are shared by all members of society, regardless of age, location, or socioeconomic background.

Artificial intelligence and big data are playing an increasingly important role in this transformation. From personalized healthcare and smart transportation to precision agriculture and environmental monitoring, these technologies are helping solve problems that were once considered intractable. When digital innovation is guided by a people-centered approach, it does not just make life more convenient — it helps build a more inclusive, efficient, and sustainable society for everyone.

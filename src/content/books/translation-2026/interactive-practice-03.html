选词填空

第 1 题 （考点：形容词+介词搭配）
The new development philosophy must be fully ______ with all aspects of economic and social work.
A consistent
B rooted
C resulted
D led
解析：be consistent with 是固定搭配，表示"与……一致"。rooted 后接 in（be rooted in），resulted 后接 in/from，led 后接 to，均不能与 with 搭配。
第 2 题 （考点：形近词辨析）
To ______ the goal of common prosperity, China needs to maintain steady economic growth while promoting social equity.
A sustain
B attain
C contain
D maintain
解析：attain 意为"实现、达到"，attain the goal 表示实现目标，符合句意。sustain 是"维持"，contain 是"包含"，maintain 是"保持"，均不如 attain 与 goal 搭配准确。
第 3 题 （考点：时政术语补全）
The concept of "______ prosperity" has become a defining goal of China's development in the new era.
A common
B shared
C mutual
D collective
解析："共同富裕"的官方译法是 common prosperity，这是固定时政术语。shared 意为"共享的"，mutual 意为"相互的"，collective 意为"集体的"，均不是标准译法。
第 4 题 （考点：词性/词形判断）
The government has introduced a series of ______ measures to support the real economy and boost market confidence.
A innovate
B innovation
C innovative
D innovatively
解析：空格修饰名词 measures，需要填入形容词。innovative（adj. 创新的）符合语法和语义。innovate 是动词，innovation 是名词，innovatively 是副词，均不能修饰 measures。
第 5 题 （考点：短语动词）
China will continue to ______ forward with supply-side structural reform to improve the quality of its economic growth.
A carry
B put
C bring
D push
解析：push forward with 是固定短语动词，表示"推进、推动"，符合句意（推进供给侧结构性改革）。carry forward 意为"发扬、推进"但不与 with 连用；put forward 意为"提出"；bring forward 意为"提前；提出"，均不符合。

High-Quality Development and Common Prosperity

China's shift from rapid growth to high-quality development marks a new stage in its economic journey. For decades, the country pursued speed and scale above all else, lifting hundreds of millions out of poverty but also creating imbalances between regions, industries, and social groups. Today, the focus is moving from "getting rich first" to "achieving common prosperity for all."

Recent policies at promoting high-quality development show that China is prioritizing quality over quantity. a more equitable distribution of development gains has become a key policy objective. These efforts have produced results: income growth for low-income groups has been faster than the national average in recent years.

One major challenge is the of balanced development in rural and inland areas. While coastal cities have rapid economic transformation, many inland regions still lag behind in industrial upgrading. To bridge this gap, the government has launched targeted programs, local industries to move up the value chain and providing more resources for education and training.

Another important factor is public of the importance of sustainable and inclusive growth. As living standards improve, people are no longer satisfied with just material wealth; they care more about social fairness and environmental quality. This changing expectation has created a strong social foundation which new development policies can be effectively carried out.

The ultimate goal is to build a society where development fruits are shared by all people. This requires not only economic restructuring but also institutional reforms in taxation, social security, and public services. When every citizen can enjoy the benefits of development fairly, common prosperity will no longer be a distant vision a tangible reality that every aspect of people's lives.

High-quality development is not about pursuing endless expansion at any cost, but about achieving growth that is balanced, coordinated, and sustainable. For a long time, many economies measured progress solely by GDP growth, ignoring issues such as income inequality, environmental degradation, and regional disparities. This narrow view of development is now being replaced by a more comprehensive approach that takes into account both economic efficiency and social equity.

The Chinese path to high-quality development recognizes that prosperity must be shared to be meaningful. It means advancing supply-side structural reform, fostering innovation-driven growth, and strengthening social safety nets so that the benefits of development can reach all segments of society. At the same time, it emphasizes the importance of regional balance, ensuring that inland areas and rural communities are not left behind in the race toward modernization.

The new development philosophy of innovative, coordinated, green, open, and shared development provides a guiding framework for this transformation. These five dimensions are not isolated goals but interconnected principles that reinforce one another. When a country can combine technological dynamism with social fairness, environmental stewardship with economic efficiency, and domestic vitality with global engagement, it has truly embarked on the road to high-quality development.

高质量发展并非要不惜一切代价追求无休止的扩张，而是要实现平衡、协调、可持续的增长。长期以来，许多经济体仅以GDP增速衡量发展，忽视了收入不平等、环境恶化和区域差距等问题。这种狭隘的发展观如今正被一种更全面的思路所取代——它既考虑经济效率，也关注社会公平。

中国的高质量发展道路认识到，繁荣只有被共享才有意义。它意味着推进供给侧结构性改革、培育创新驱动增长、加强社会保障体系，使发展成果能够惠及社会各阶层。同时，它强调区域平衡的重要性，确保内陆地区和农村社区在现代化进程中不被落下。

创新、协调、绿色、开放、共享的新发展理念为这一转型提供了指导框架。这五个维度并非孤立的目标，而是相互促进、相互支撑的原则。当一个国家能够将技术活力与社会公平相结合，将生态治理与经济效率相结合，将国内活力与全球参与相结合时，它才真正走上了高质量发展之路。

共同富裕是中国特色社会主义的本质要求。近年来，中国在推动高质量发展的同时，不断加大保障和改善民生的力度。脱贫攻坚战取得全面胜利，近一亿农村贫困人口实现脱贫，为全球减贫事业作出了重大贡献。

共同富裕不是少数人的富裕，也不是整齐划一的平均主义，而是全体人民的富裕。各地因地制宜探索共同富裕的实践路径，通过发展产业、促进就业、完善分配等方式，不断缩小城乡差距和收入差距。与此同时，中国积极构建初次分配、再分配、第三次分配协调配套的制度体系。

值得一提的是，共同富裕是一个长期目标，需要全体人民共同努力。我们既要做大"蛋糕"，也要分好"蛋糕"，让每个人都能在发展中找到自己的位置，共享改革发展的成果。可以说，共同富裕不仅是经济问题，也是关系党的执政基础的重大政治问题。

Common prosperity is an essential requirement of socialism with Chinese characteristics. In recent years, while promoting high-quality development, China has continuously intensified efforts to safeguard and improve people's livelihoods. The battle against poverty has won a comprehensive victory, with nearly 100 million rural poor lifted out of poverty, making a major contribution to the global cause of poverty reduction.

Localities have explored practical paths to common prosperity according to local conditions, continuously narrowing the urban-rural gap and income gap through industrial development, employment promotion, and improved distribution. Meanwhile, China has actively built an institutional system featuring coordinated and complementary primary distribution, redistribution, and tertiary distribution.

It is worth mentioning that common prosperity is a long-term goal that requires the joint efforts of all people. We must both make the "cake" bigger and divide it properly, so that everyone can find their place in development and share the fruits of reform and development. It can be said that common prosperity is not only an economic issue but also a major political issue concerning the Party's governance foundation.

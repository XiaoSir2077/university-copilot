选词填空
共 5 题，每题 2 分，共 10 分
第 1 题 （考点：动词+介词搭配）
China is committed ______ the goal of peak carbon emissions before 2030 and carbon neutrality before 2060.
A to
B with
C for
D of
解析：be committed to 是固定搭配，表示"致力于、承诺"，to 是介词，后接名词或动名词。
第 2 题 （考点：形近词辨析）
The rapid growth of renewable energy has significantly ______ the country's energy structure in recent years.
A assumed
B consumed
C resumed
D transformed
解析：transform 意为"改变、转变"，transform the energy structure 表示改变能源结构，符合句意。assume 是"假设；承担"，consume 是"消费"，resume 是"恢复"，均不符合语境。
第 3 题 （考点：时政术语补全）
The concept of "______ civilization" has been written into the Constitution of the People's Republic of China.
A environmental
B ecological
C green
D natural
解析："生态文明"的官方译法是 ecological civilization，这是固定时政术语。environmental 是"环境的"，green 是"绿色的"，natural 是"自然的"，均不是标准译法。
第 4 题 （考点：词性/词形判断）
The ______ growth of electric vehicles has led to a noticeable drop in urban air pollution levels.
A accelerate
B acceleration
C accelerated
D accelerating
解析：空格修饰名词 growth，需要填入形容词。accelerated（adj. 加速的）符合语法和语义。accelerate 是动词，acceleration 是名词，accelerating 是现在分词作形容词时通常表主动正在进行的动作，此处用 accelerated 更合适，表"已加速的增长"。
第 5 题 （考点：短语动词）
Many traditional factories have had to ______ with stricter environmental regulations or face closure.
A come up
B put up
C keep up
D make up
解析：keep up with 是固定短语动词，表示"跟上、不落后于"，符合句意（跟上更严格的环保法规）。come up with 意为"想出、提出"；put up with 意为"忍受"；make up with 意为"与……和解"，均不符合。

Ecological Civilization and Green Development

China's pursuit of ecological civilization represents a fundamental shift in development philosophy. For many years, rapid economic growth often came at the expense of the environment, with air pollution, water contamination, and soil degradation becoming increasingly pressing issues. Today, however, the country is redefining progress by placing environmental protection at the core of its national strategy.

Recent policies at promoting green development show that China is moving away from the old model of "pollute first, clean up later." a low-carbon economy has become a national priority, with substantial investments flowing into solar, wind, and hydropower projects. These efforts have produced results: China is now the world's largest producer of renewable energy.

One key challenge is the of green technology in rural and less developed regions. While coastal cities have embraced clean energy, many inland areas still heavily on coal for electricity and heating. To bridge this gap, the government has launched special programs, local enterprises to adopt eco-friendly practices and providing financial support for green transition.

Another important factor is public of environmental protection. As living standards improve, people are no longer satisfied with just economic growth; they demand cleaner air, safer drinking water, and healthier food. This growing awareness has created a strong social foundation which green policies can be effectively implemented.

The ultimate goal is to build a beautiful China where humans coexist harmoniously with nature. This requires not only technological innovation and policy support but also a change in lifestyle and consumption patterns. When every citizen understands that ecological protection is to their own well-being, sustainable development will no longer be a distant vision a daily reality shared by all.

Ecological civilization is not about sacrificing economic growth for environmental protection, but about finding a balanced path where nature and development reinforce each other. For decades, many developing countries faced a dilemma: rapid industrialization often led to severe pollution and resource depletion, while strict environmental rules were seen as a barrier to poverty alleviation. This false dichotomy between the economy and the environment has now been challenged by a new approach.

The Chinese concept of ecological civilization recognizes that protecting the environment is itself a form of productive investment. It means promoting green industries, circular economy models, and low-carbon technologies so that economic growth can continue without exceeding ecological limits. At the same time, it emphasizes the preservation of biodiversity and natural landscapes, ensuring that future generations will inherit a planet that is not only richer but also cleaner and more beautiful.

International cooperation is essential in this endeavor. Climate change, ocean pollution, and species extinction are global challenges that no single country can solve alone. The Belt and Road Initiative, with its emphasis on green development, has provided a platform for countries to share clean energy technologies, jointly protect transboundary ecosystems, and work together toward a sustainable future for all.

生态文明并非要为了环境保护而牺牲经济增长，而是要找到一条自然与发展相互促进的平衡之路。几十年来，许多发展中国家面临一个两难困境：快速工业化往往导致严重污染和资源枯竭，而严格的环保法规又被视为减贫的障碍。这种经济与环境之间的虚假二元对立，如今正受到一种新思路的挑战。

中国的生态文明理念认识到，保护环境本身就是一种生产性投资。它意味着推动绿色产业、循环经济模式和低碳技术，使经济增长能够在不突破生态极限的前提下持续进行。同时，它强调保护生物多样性和自然景观，确保子孙后代继承的不仅是一个更富裕的星球，也是一个更清洁、更美丽的星球。

国际合作在这一事业中至关重要。气候变化、海洋污染和物种灭绝是全球性挑战，没有哪个国家能够独自解决。"一带一路"倡议以绿色发展为重点，为各国分享清洁能源技术、共同保护跨境生态系统、携手迈向人类可持续未来提供了平台。

值得一提的是，碳达峰碳中和目标的提出，展现了中国应对气候变化的坚定决心。中国承诺力争2030年前实现碳达峰、2060年前实现碳中和，这不仅是中国可持续发展的内在要求，也是构建人类命运共同体的责任担当。

China attaches great importance to ecological civilization development and has incorporated it into the overall national development plan. In recent years, China has made remarkable achievements in ecological and environmental protection: energy consumption per unit of GDP has continued to decline, the proportion of clean energy has kept increasing, forest coverage has grown steadily, and air quality has improved significantly.

The concept that lucid waters and lush mountains are invaluable assets has taken deep root among the people. Local authorities have actively explored mechanisms for realizing the value of ecological products and promoted the development of green industries such as eco-tourism and under-forest economy, turning a sound ecological environment into a growth point for people's better lives. Meanwhile, China has actively participated in global environmental governance and made important contributions to addressing climate change.

It is worth mentioning that the proposal of the peak carbon emissions and carbon neutrality goals demonstrates China's firm determination in tackling climate change. China has pledged to strive to peak carbon dioxide emissions before 2030 and achieve carbon neutrality before 2060. This is not only an inherent requirement for China's sustainable development but also a sense of responsibility for building a community with a shared future for mankind.

选词填空

第 1 题 （考点：时政术语理解）
"Chinese-style modernization" refers to the modernization pursued under the leadership of the Communist Party of China, which ______ both elements of commonality and distinct Chinese characteristics.
A integrates
B interacts
C interprets
D intervenes
解析：integrate 意为"融合、结合"，integrates both commonality and Chinese characteristics 表示融合了共性和中国特色，符合句意。interact 是"互动"，interpret 是"解释"，intervene 是"干预"，均不符合。
第 2 题 （考点：动词+介词搭配）
Chinese-style modernization has much to offer ______ the cause of global human progress and the building of a better world.
A for
B to
C in
D toward
解析：offer to 是固定搭配，表示"为……做贡献、对……有用"。offer much to the cause 表示为这一事业贡献良多。
第 3 题 （考点：形近词辨析）
The Chinese path to modernization demonstrates that each nation can ______ its own development model based on its specific national conditions and cultural traditions.
A forge
B forget
C form
D force
解析：forge 意为"锻造、打造"，forge its own development model 表示打造自己的发展模式，比 form（形成）更具力度和主动性，符合语境。forget 是"忘记"，force 是"强迫"，均不符合。
第 4 题 （考点：词性/词形判断）
The ______ process of common prosperity is an essential feature of Chinese-style modernization and distinguishes it from Western modernization.
A pursue
B pursuit
C pursued
D pursuing
解析：空格前有定冠词 the，后有介词 of，需要填入名词。pursuit（n. 追求）the pursuit of common prosperity 表示"共同富裕的追求过程"，符合语法和语义。pursue 是动词，pursued 是过去分词，pursuing 是动名词但作定语时不如 pursuit 正式。
第 5 题 （考点：短语动词）
As China continues to ______ forward on the path of modernization, it remains committed to peaceful development and win-win cooperation with other countries.
A march
B walk
C step
D move
解析：march forward 是固定表达，表示"阔步前进、奋勇前进"，常用于正式时政语境，带有坚定有力的意味。walk forward 太平淡，step forward 是"向前迈步"，move forward 是"前进"但力度不如 march。

Chinese-Style Modernization: A Path of Its Own

Modernization has long been seen as a universal process that all societies must follow, often modeled closely on Western experiences. However, China's remarkable development over the past decades has shown that there is more than one path to modernization. Chinese-style modernization represents a distinct approach that combines universal trends with national realities, new possibilities for developing countries seeking their own paths forward.

Recent studies the key features of Chinese-style modernization highlight five defining characteristics: a huge population, common prosperity for all, material and cultural-ethical advancement, harmony between humanity and nature, and peaceful development. on its own historical conditions and cultural heritage, China has managed to achieve in just a few decades what took Western countries centuries to accomplish. These achievements have produced results: hundreds of millions have been lifted out of poverty, and the country has become the world's second largest economy.

One major challenge is the of balanced development across different regions and among different groups. While coastal cities have experienced rapid growth and modernization, inland and rural areas still face significant gaps in living standards and public services. To address this imbalance, the government has introduced a series of coordinated development strategies, investment in less developed regions and implementing targeted poverty alleviation programs.

Another important factor is public of the importance of both material progress and cultural-ethical advancement. In the early stages of reform and opening up, the emphasis was largely on economic growth. Today, there is a growing recognition that modernization must include cultural confidence and spiritual enrichment, creating a more balanced society which people can thrive in both material and cultural terms.

Chinese-style modernization is not a rejection of Western experiences but a creative adaptation based on China's own realities. When a country can learn from others while staying true to itself, modernization will no longer be a single-track race a diverse global journey that all nations.

Chinese-style modernization is not about copying Western models wholesale, but about finding a path that suits China's own conditions while learning from the experiences of others. For a long time, modernization was often equated with Westernization, and countries that deviated from the Western path were seen as backward or abnormal. This narrow understanding is now being challenged by China's success, which demonstrates that there are multiple pathways to modernization, each shaped by a nation's unique history, culture, and social conditions.

The Chinese model of modernization recognizes that development must be people-centered and inclusive. It means pursuing economic growth while ensuring social equity, promoting material progress while advancing cultural-ethical progress, and pursuing industrialization while protecting the ecological environment. At the same time, it emphasizes the importance of peaceful development, ensuring that China's rise does not come at the expense of other countries but contributes to the common prosperity of all nations.

The significance of Chinese-style modernization extends far beyond China's borders. It offers a new choice for developing countries that wish to modernize while preserving their independence and cultural identity. When every nation is free to choose its own path to development, modernization will not just benefit a privileged few — it will become a shared global endeavor that brings progress and prosperity to all of humanity.

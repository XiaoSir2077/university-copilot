第 1 题 （考点：短语动词）
With the rapid aging of the population, more and more seniors are looking ______ new ways to stay active and engaged in society.
A up
B for
C after
D into
解析：look for 是固定短语动词，表示"寻找"，符合句意（寻找新方式）。look up 意为"查阅；好转"，look after 意为"照顾"，look into 意为"调查"，均不符合。
第 2 题 （考点：形近词辨析）
The government has introduced various policies to ______ the development of the silver economy and improve the quality of life for the elderly.
A promote
B prompt
C proceed
D propose
解析：promote 意为"促进、推动"，promote the development 表示促进发展，符合句意。prompt 是"促使；提示"，proceed 是"继续进行"，propose 是"提议"，均不如 promote 准确。
第 3 题 （考点：近义词语境辨析）
Many cities are now ______ their public facilities to make them more senior-friendly, such as installing handrails and lowering bus steps.
A altering
B transforming
C adapting
D varying
解析：adapt 意为"改造、使适应"，adapt facilities 表示改造设施以适应老年人需求，符合语境。alter 是"改变"但不强调"适应"；transform 是"彻底改造"，程度过重；vary 是"变化"，均不合适。
第 4 题 （考点：词性/词形判断）
The ______ growth of the elderly population has created both challenges and opportunities for China's social and economic development.
A accelerate
B acceleration
C accelerated
D accelerating
解析：空格修饰名词 growth，需要填入形容词。accelerating（adj. 正在加速的）表示持续进行的动作，符合"人口老龄化加速增长"的语境。accelerate 是动词，acceleration 是名词，accelerated 是"已加速的"，表示已完成的状态。
第 5 题 （考点：动词+介词搭配）
As people live longer, they need to ______ for their retirement years both financially and emotionally.
A prepare
B plan
C provide
D account
解析：prepare for 是固定搭配，表示"为……做准备"，符合句意（为退休生活做准备）。plan 后接 for 时通常 plan for sth 表"为某事做计划"，但不如 prepare for 常用和准确；provide for 意为"供养"；account for 意为"占比；解释"。

The Rise of the Silver Economy

China's aging population is not just a demographic challenge; it is also giving birth to a vast and growing market known as the "silver economy." With more than 280 million people aged 60 and above, China has the largest elderly population in the world. This demographic shift is reshaping industries from healthcare and eldercare to tourism and education, new business opportunities along the way.

Recent studies the spending patterns of senior citizens show that today's elderly are no longer passive recipients of care. more active lifestyles and higher disposable incomes, they are willing to spend on products and services that improve their quality of life. These changes have produced results: the silver economy is expected to account for more than 30 percent of GDP by 2050.

One major challenge is the of standardized eldercare services in many rural and small-town areas. While big cities boast modern nursing homes and home care platforms, regions often lack even basic senior-friendly facilities. To address this gap, the government has launched a national action plan, local communities to develop home-based care services and training more professional caregivers.

Another important factor is public of the economic potential of the aging population. For a long time, aging was seen purely as a burden rather than an opportunity. This perception is gradually changing, creating a more supportive environment which innovative silver economy businesses can thrive.

The silver economy is not just about providing for the elderly; it is about creating a society where people of all ages can lead dignified and fulfilling lives. When aging is no longer feared but embraced as a natural stage of life, the silver economy will no longer be a niche market a mainstream force that drives inclusive growth.

The silver economy is not about selling products to a passive group of consumers, but about building an inclusive ecosystem that respects the dignity and diversity of older adults. For many years, the elderly were often portrayed as frail and dependent, with products designed for them focusing mainly on healthcare and basic necessities. This narrow view of aging is now being challenged by a new generation of seniors who are healthier, more educated, and more willing to spend on experiences and self-improvement.

The rise of the silver economy recognizes that older people are not just recipients of social support but also important contributors to economic and social life. It means developing age-friendly communities, promoting lifelong learning, and creating more employment opportunities for seniors who wish to remain active in the workforce. At the same time, it emphasizes the need for better pension systems and long-term care insurance, ensuring that every senior citizen can enjoy their later years with security and dignity.

Technology is playing an increasingly important role in this transformation. From smart healthcare devices and telemedicine platforms to social apps designed specifically for the elderly, digital innovations are helping bridge the digital divide and making life more convenient for older people. When technology is designed with senior users in mind, it does not just make life easier — it helps maintain social connections, preserve mental agility, and foster a greater sense of independence.

银发经济并非要向一群被动的消费者推销产品，而是要构建一个尊重老年人尊严与多样性的包容性生态系统。长期以来，老年人常常被描绘成虚弱、依赖他人的形象，为他们设计的产品也主要集中在医疗和基本生活用品上。如今，这种对老龄化的狭隘看法正受到新一代老年人的挑战——他们更健康、受教育程度更高，也更愿意为体验和自我提升付费。

银发经济的兴起认识到，老年人不仅是社会支持的接受者，也是经济和社会生活的重要贡献者。它意味着建设老年友好型社区、推动终身学习、为希望继续工作的老年人创造更多就业机会。同时，它强调完善养老金体系和长期护理保险的必要性，确保每一位老年人都能有保障、有尊严地安享晚年。

人口老龄化是中国未来发展面临的重要课题。截至2025年底，中国60岁及以上人口已超过2.8亿，占总人口的比例接近20%。积极应对人口老龄化，不仅关系到亿万老年人的福祉，也关系到经济社会的高质量发展。

值得一提的是，积极老龄化的理念正在深入人心。越来越多的老年人不再满足于在家养老，而是选择走出家门，参与社区活动、学习新技能、投身志愿服务。可以说，银发经济不仅为经济增长注入了新动能，也让老年人找到了新的人生价值和社会归属感。

Population aging is an important issue facing China's future development. By the end of 2025, the number of people aged 60 and above in China had exceeded 280 million, accounting for nearly 20 percent of the total population. Proactively responding to population aging is not only related to the well-being of hundreds of millions of elderly people but also to the high-quality development of the economy and society.

The silver economy is becoming a new growth point. Local authorities have actively developed silver industries such as eldercare services, healthcare, and cultural tourism, continuously meeting the diverse and multi-level needs of the elderly. Meanwhile, smart eldercare has developed rapidly, with new models such as smart bracelets, remote medical consultations, and online courses for senior universities emerging one after another, making life for the elderly more convenient and colorful.

It is worth mentioning that the concept of active aging is taking deep root among the people. An increasing number of seniors are no longer satisfied with aging at home; instead, they choose to step out, participate in community activities, learn new skills, and engage in volunteer services. It can be said that the silver economy has not only injected new momentum into economic growth but also helped older people find new meaning in life and a greater sense of social belonging.

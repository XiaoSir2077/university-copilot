第 1 题 （考点：动词+介词搭配）
All local governments must ______ the national strategy of rural revitalization and prioritize agricultural development.
A comply with
B abide in
C adhere on
D conform for
解析：comply with 是固定搭配，表示"遵守、服从"。abide 后接 by（abide by），adhere 后接 to（adhere to），conform 后接 to（conform to）。本题只有 A 选项搭配正确。
第 2 题 （考点：形近词辨析）
The remarkable progress in poverty reduction is often ______ to the targeted policies implemented over the past decade.
A contributed
B distributed
C attributed
D substituted
解析：attribute sth. to sth. 表示"把……归因于"，本句用被动语态 be attributed to，意为"（进步）归因于政策"。contribute to 是"促成、贡献"，主语通常是原因；distribute 是"分配"；substitute 是"替代"，均不符合句意。
第 3 题 （考点：时政术语补全）
The vision of "a community with a ______ future for mankind" has been written into many international documents.
A common
B shared
C mutual
D joint
解析："人类命运共同体"的官方译法是 a community with a shared future for mankind，这是固定时政术语。common 意为"共同的、普遍的"，mutual 意为"相互的"，joint 意为"联合的"，均不是标准译法。
第 4 题 （考点：词性/词形判断）
The ______ use of digital technologies has transformed traditional farming into smart agriculture.
A widely
B widen
C widespread
D width
解析：空格修饰名词 use，需要填入形容词。widespread（adj. 广泛的）符合语法和语义。widely 是副词，widen 是动词，width 是名词，均不能修饰 use。
第 5 题 （考点：短语动词）
Improved infrastructure and e-commerce platforms ______ for over 30 percent of the increase in rural incomes last year.
A accounted
B called
C brought
D made
解析：account for 是固定短语动词，表示"（在数量、比例上）占"，符合句意（占农村收入增长的 30% 以上）。call for 意为"要求、需要"；bring for 不构成固定短语；make for 意为"走向、导致"，均不符合。

Rural Revitalization and Cultural Heritage

The campaign for rural revitalization is not merely about economic growth; it is also about preserving the cultural identity of countless villages. As young people migrate to cities, many traditional crafts and folk customs face the risk of disappearing. However, experience suggests that the best protection is not a fence around old buildings, but a sustainable purpose that keeps traditions alive in daily life.

Recent studies the relationship between cultural heritage preservation and rural development show that villages with strong cultural programs attract more tourists and retain more young residents. cultural resources can create new economic opportunities while strengthening local identity. For example, traditional handcrafts can be developed into cultural products, and old houses can be transformed into guesthouses without losing their original charm.

One major challenge is the of skilled craftspeople. Many elderly artisans have invaluable techniques, but there are not enough young people willing to learn. To address this, some local governments have launched training programs, young villagers to master traditional skills and become cultural inheritors. These efforts have produced results in several pilot areas.

Another factor is the of financial support. While many projects start with great enthusiasm, they often struggle to secure long-term funding. Sustainable rural development must therefore on a diversified funding model that combines government investment, private capital, and community resources.

The ultimate goal is to create villages where people do not merely for better urban lives, but choose to stay because they see a bright future at home. This requires not only material improvements but also a renewed sense of pride and belonging. When rural culture thrives, the countryside becomes a place young people are eager to return to and urban residents are curious about.

Rural revitalization is not about transplanting urban models into the countryside, but about unlocking the intrinsic value of rural communities. For decades, development policies often treated villages as backward spaces waiting to be modernized, overlooking the fact that many rural areas possess unique ecological, cultural, and social assets.

The new approach recognizes that sustainable rural development must be people-centered. It means improving infrastructure, public services, and digital connectivity so that rural residents can enjoy a quality of life comparable to that in cities. At the same time, it emphasizes the preservation of traditional villages, local dialects, and handicraft skills, ensuring that modernization does not come at the cost of cultural extinction.

A sense of identity and belonging is crucial in this process. When young people feel connected to their hometowns, they are more likely to return after graduation and contribute their knowledge and energy. In this way, rural revitalization becomes a two-way journey: the countryside gains new vitality, while young people find meaningful opportunities closer to home.

乡村振兴并非把城市模式移植到农村，而是要释放乡村社区的内在价值。几十年来，发展政策常常把村庄视为等待现代化的落后空间，忽视了许多农村地区拥有独特的生态、文化和社会资源这一事实。

新思路认识到，可持续的农村发展必须以人为本。这意味着改善基础设施、公共服务和数字连接，让农村居民能够享受与城市相当的生活品质。同时，它强调保护传统村落、地方方言和手工艺技能，确保现代化不以文化消亡为代价。

认同感和归属感在这一过程中至关重要。当年轻人感到与家乡有联结时，他们更可能在毕业后返乡，贡献自己的知识和精力。这样一来，乡村振兴便成为一场双向旅程：农村获得新的活力，年轻人也在离家更近的地方找到有意义的机会。

近年来，中国大力推进乡村振兴战略，农业农村现代化取得显著成效。数据显示，农村居民人均可支配收入持续增长，乡村基础设施明显改善，教育、医疗等公共服务水平不断提升。

乡村振兴的关键在于产业兴旺。各地因地制宜发展特色农业、乡村旅游和农村电商，为农民创造了更多就业机会。与此同时，生态保护也被放在重要位置，绿水青山就是金山银山的理念深入人心。

值得注意的是，人才振兴是乡村振兴的重要支撑。越来越多的大学生和返乡青年投身乡村建设，他们用新知识、新技术为农村发展注入活力。可以说，乡村振兴不仅改变了农村面貌，也为年轻人提供了实现梦想的广阔舞台。

📖 参考译文

In recent years, China has vigorously advanced the rural revitalization strategy, achieving remarkable progress in agricultural and rural modernization. Data show that rural residents' per capita disposable income has continued to grow, rural infrastructure has been significantly improved, and the quality of public services such as education and medical care has been steadily enhanced.

The key to rural revitalization lies in thriving industries. Localities have developed characteristic agriculture, rural tourism, and e-commerce according to local conditions, creating more employment opportunities for farmers. Meanwhile, ecological protection has also been given high priority, and the concept that lucid waters and lush mountains are invaluable assets has taken deep root among the people.

It is worth mentioning that talent revitalization is an important pillar of rural revitalization. An increasing number of university graduates and returning young people have devoted themselves to rural construction, injecting vitality into rural development with new knowledge and technologies. It can be said that rural revitalization has not only transformed the countryside but also provided young people with a broad stage to realize their dreams.

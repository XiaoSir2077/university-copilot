# 基础练习题样章（一）：初高中衔接

> 用途：可直接打印给学生做，也可拆成随堂小测。
> 范围：不绑定教材版本，覆盖 be 动词、代词、冠词、名词复数、介词、四大基础时态、there be、祈使句、疑问句。
> 教材版本确认后，会按每个单元的词汇和语法点出**配套题**，这套是给基础薄弱学生补地基用的。
> 建议用时：30 分钟。答案与解析在最后，做题前请翻过去。

## Part 1 单项选择（每题 2 分，共 60 分）

**1. My name \_\_\_ Li Ming. I \_\_\_ a student.**
A. is; am　　B. am; is　　C. are; am　　D. be; am

**2. There \_\_\_ a pen and two books on the desk.**
A. is　　B. are　　C. have　　D. has

**3. The students in our class \_\_\_ hard-working.**
A. is　　B. are　　C. am　　D. be

**4. \_\_\_ is my aunt. \_\_\_ is a teacher.**
A. He; She　　B. She; She　　C. Her; She　　D. She; Her

**5. This book is not \_\_\_. It's \_\_\_.**
A. my; her　　B. mine; hers　　C. me; she　　D. I; hers

**6. — Is this \_\_\_ ruler?　— No, it's not mine.**
A. you　　B. your　　C. yours　　D. yourself

**7. I go to school at 7:30 in \_\_\_ morning.**
A. a　　B. an　　C. the　　D. /

**8. She is \_\_\_ English teacher, and she works in \_\_\_ middle school.**
A. an; a　　B. a; an　　C. the; a　　D. an; /

**9. I usually play \_\_\_ basketball after school.**
A. a　　B. the　　C. /　　D. an

**10. There are some \_\_\_ and three \_\_\_ in the picture.**
A. sheeps; childs　　B. sheep; children　　C. sheep; childs　　D. sheeps; children

**11. Today is \_\_\_ Day.**
A. Teacher's　　B. Teachers'　　C. Teachers　　D. the Teachers

**12. I'm hungry. Please give me two \_\_\_.**
A. bread　　B. breads　　C. pieces of bread　　D. piece of bread

**13. My birthday is \_\_\_ October 15th.**
A. in　　B. on　　C. at　　D. for

**14. We have lunch \_\_\_ 12:00 every day.**
A. in　　B. on　　C. at　　D. of

**15. The cat is \_\_\_ the chair. I can't see it.**
A. on　　B. under　　C. between　　D. of

**16. She \_\_\_ up at six every morning.**
A. get　　B. gets　　C. got　　D. getting

**17. My father \_\_\_ TV in the evening.**
A. don't watch　　B. doesn't watch　　C. doesn't watches　　D. isn't watch

**18. — \_\_\_ you often late for school?　— No, I \_\_\_.**
A. Do; don't　　B. Are; don't　　C. Are; am not　　D. Do; am not

**19. Look! The boys \_\_\_ football on the playground.**
A. play　　B. plays　　C. are playing　　D. is playing

**20. Be quiet! The baby \_\_\_.**
A. sleeps　　B. sleep　　C. is sleeping　　D. are sleeping

**21. I \_\_\_ a very interesting book yesterday.**
A. read　　B. reads　　C. am reading　　D. will read

**22. She \_\_\_ to Beijing last summer.**
A. go　　B. goes　　C. went　　D. goed

**23. — \_\_\_ you at home last night?　— Yes, I \_\_\_.**
A. Were; was　　B. Did; did　　C. Were; were　　D. Did; was

**24. We \_\_\_ a test next Monday.**
A. have　　B. had　　C. will have　　D. are having

**25. Look at the clouds! It \_\_\_ rain.**
A. will　　B. is going to　　C. going to　　D. /

**26. There \_\_\_ a meeting tomorrow afternoon.**
A. is going to be　　B. will have　　C. is going to have　　D. are going to be

**27. \_\_\_ late for class again, Tom.**
A. Not be　　B. Don't be　　C. Isn't　　D. Doesn't be

**28. — \_\_\_ is that man?　— He's my uncle.**
A. What　　B. Who　　C. Whose　　D. How

**29. — \_\_\_ apples do you want?　— Five, please.**
A. How much　　B. How many　　C. How long　　D. How old

**30. My mother often helps me \_\_\_ my English.**
A. with　　B. in　　C. on　　D. at

## Part 2 用所给词的适当形式填空（每题 4 分，共 40 分）

**1.** There \_\_\_\_\_\_ (be) some water in the bottle.

**2.** Listen! Who \_\_\_\_\_\_ (sing) in the next room?

**3.** He usually \_\_\_\_\_\_ (walk) to school, but yesterday he \_\_\_\_\_\_ (take) a bus.

**4.** The two \_\_\_\_\_\_ (woman) over there are our \_\_\_\_\_\_ (teacher).

**5.** \_\_\_\_\_\_ (not open) the window, please. It's cold.

**6.** My sister is good at \_\_\_\_\_\_ (dance).

**7.** There \_\_\_\_\_\_ (be) a football match on TV tomorrow evening.

**8.** These books are not mine. Are they \_\_\_\_\_\_ (you)?

**9.** She \_\_\_\_\_\_ (buy) a new dress last weekend.

**10.** Look! The children \_\_\_\_\_\_ (fly) kites in the park. They \_\_\_\_\_\_ (look) very happy.

## 答案与解析

### Part 1

| 题 | 答案 | 解析 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A | my name 用 is；I 用 am。 |
| 2 | A | there be 句型遵循就近原则，离 be 最近的 a pen 是单数，用 is。 |
| 3 | B | 主语 the students 是复数，用 are。 |
| 4 | B | 两处都作主语，用主格 She。 |
| 5 | B | 两空后都没有名词，用名词性物主代词 mine / hers。 |
| 6 | B | 空后有名词 ruler，用形容词性物主代词 your。 |
| 7 | C | 固定搭配 in the morning。 |
| 8 | A | English 以元音音素开头用 an；middle 以辅音音素开头用 a。 |
| 9 | C | 球类运动前不加冠词：play basketball。 |
| 10 | B | sheep 单复数同形；child 的复数是不规则变化 children。 |
| 11 | B | 教师节是复数所有格 Teachers' Day。 |
| 12 | C | bread 不可数，"两片面包"用 two pieces of bread。 |
| 13 | B | 具体某一天前用 on。 |
| 14 | C | 具体钟点前用 at。 |
| 15 | B | "看不见猫"说明猫在椅子下面，用 under。 |
| 16 | B | every morning 表习惯，用一般现在时；主语 she 第三人称单数加 -s。 |
| 17 | B | 一般现在时否定：doesn't + 动词原形。 |
| 18 | C | be late for 是固定短语；主语 you 用 Are，回答 I 用 am。 |
| 19 | C | Look! 提示现在进行时；the boys 复数，用 are playing。 |
| 20 | C | Be quiet! 提示动作正在发生；the baby 单数，用 is sleeping。 |
| 21 | A | yesterday 用一般过去时；read 的过去式拼写仍为 read（读音不同）。 |
| 22 | C | last summer 用过去式，go 的过去式是 went。 |
| 23 | A | at home 用 be 动词；you 配 Were，I 配 was。 |
| 24 | C | next Monday 用一般将来时 will + 动词原形。 |
| 25 | B | 有迹象（乌云）表明即将发生，用 be going to。 |
| 26 | A | there be 的将来时是 there is going to be / there will be，不能与 have 混用。 |
| 27 | B | 否定祈使句：Don't + 动词原形（be）。 |
| 28 | B | 答语是身份（my uncle），问"是谁"用 Who。 |
| 29 | B | apples 可数且答语是数量，用 How many。 |
| 30 | A | 固定搭配 help sb. with sth.（在某方面帮助某人）。 |

### Part 2

| 题 | 答案 | 解析 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | is | water 不可数名词，按单数处理。 |
| 2 | is singing | Listen! 提示现在进行时；who 视作单数。 |
| 3 | walks; took | usually 配一般现在时（三单）；yesterday 配一般过去时。 |
| 4 | women; teachers | 两个名词都要变复数；woman 的复数是 women。 |
| 5 | Don't open | 否定祈使句 Don't + 动词原形。 |
| 6 | dancing | be good at 中 at 是介词，后接动词 -ing 形式。 |
| 7 | will be | tomorrow evening 表将来，there be 将来时用 there will be。 |
| 8 | yours | 空后无名词，用名词性物主代词 yours（=your books）。 |
| 9 | bought | last weekend 用一般过去时，buy 的过去式是 bought。 |
| 10 | are flying; look | Look! 用现在进行时（children 复数）；"看起来开心"是状态，用一般现在时 look。 |

## 讲评建议（给老师）

- 错误率最高的预计是 **2、26 题（there be 与 have 混用）**和 **21 题（read 过去式）**，建议板书对比讲一次。
- Part 2 的第 3、10 题一题考两个时态，是给中等生的区分题；全班正确率低于一半就再出 5 道同型题当堂巩固。
